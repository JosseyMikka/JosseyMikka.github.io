Technology in context

research & projects

My research focuses on sustainable, inclusive technology education and how learning tools can respond to local conditions in Namibia.

Doctoral research · ongoing

Sustainable technology education in Namibia

Using Design Science Research to investigate contextually relevant approaches to STEM learning for young learners, with attention to resources, culture, participation and long-term use.

The work brings educational robotics, AI-supported learning and practical teaching activities into a broader research programme.

Prototype · ongoing research

STEMMate

An AI-supported project exploring curriculum-aligned STEM learning and teacher support in Namibia. Current design priorities include useful explanations, progressive support, learner independence and teacher review.

Research & development

Educational robotics

Investigating robotics activities as a setting for programming, construction and STEM learning. Work with a Teknologiskolen camp examines learner familiarity and support needs, with implications for a future adaptation in Namibia.

The camp manuscript is exploratory; survey familiarity is not presented as proof of individual learning gains.

Research activity · ongoing

Offline-first information retrieval

Exploring school-local retrieval of curriculum-aligned STEM resources when connectivity is intermittent and devices are shared. This work informed SIGIR ’26 presentation materials.

Related engagement includes SIGIR Futures for Africa discussions on a shared African information retrieval research agenda.

Framework development

STTF-N

The Sustainable Technology Teaching Framework for Namibia is an ongoing effort to support practical, context-sensitive decisions about technology teaching.

Public framework version, evaluation summary and reusable resources: to be confirmed.
